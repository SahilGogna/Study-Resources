# 30 Day LinkedIn (Resource Doc)
---

## Why This Works

- LinkedIn's algorithm rewards consistent activity, not one-off bursts
- Recruiters are active on LinkedIn daily — especially at banks, consulting firms, and tech companies
- Most job seekers do only passive activity (scrolling, applying). Active users get noticed.
- 20 minutes a day is all this takes

---

## Before You Start — Profile Checklist

Do this once before starting the 30-day challenge:

- [ ]  Profile photo: professional, clear background, good lighting
- [ ]  Banner image: custom or related to your field
- [ ]  Headline: not just your job title. Include what you do and who you help. Example: "Data Analyst | SQL & Python | Helping companies turn data into decisions"
- [ ]  About section: 3–5 sentences. Who you are, what you do, what you are looking for. End with a call to action.
- [ ]  Experience: bullet points, not paragraphs. Start with action verbs. Include numbers.
- [ ]  Skills: add at least 15 relevant skills. Endorse others and they will endorse you back.
- [ ]  Open to Work: turn on (visible to recruiters only is fine if you are currently employed)

## The Daily 3-Action Routine (20 minutes)

### Action 1 — Post Once (5–7 minutes)

Post something every single day. It does not need to be long.

**Easy post ideas for tech job seekers:**

- One thing you learned today (tool, concept, industry news)
- A resource you found helpful (with your take on it)
- A question for your network ("What SQL concept took you longest to understand?")
- A small win (completed a certification, finished a project)
- A repost of someone else's content with your own 2-3 sentence comment
- A career update (started a course, attended an event, reached a milestone)

**Post format that works:**

- Line 1: Hook (bold claim or question)
- Lines 2-5: The point in short bullets or sentences
- Last line: Question or CTA ("What do you think?", "Drop a comment below")
- No hashtag spam — 2-3 relevant hashtags maximum

### Action 2 — Send 2 Connection Requests (5 minutes)

Do not just click Connect. Send a personalized note — every time.

**Who to connect with:**

- Recruiters at companies you want to work at
- People in your target role at your target company
- Hiring managers at companies of interest
- Alumni from your university now working in your target market
- People you met at events (ORU sessions, meetups, webinars)

**Important — LinkedIn's character limit:** Connection notes are capped at 200 characters. Anything longer gets cut off before your contact even sees it, so keep every note short, specific, and personal.

## Personalized Connection Request Templates (200 characters max)

Each one below is under 200 characters, so you can paste it straight in and just swap the brackets.

> Hi [Name], I'm exploring [Role] roles and came across your profile. Would love to connect and learn from your experience.
> 

*(131 characters)*

> Hi [Name], I noticed you work in [Field] at [Company]. I'm targeting similar roles and would value connecting with you.
> 

*(119 characters)*

> Hi [Name], we're both [University] alumni. I'm building my network in [Field] and would love to connect with you.
> 

*(113 characters)*

> Hi [Name], great meeting you at [Event]. Would love to stay connected as I continue my [Field] job search.
> 

*(109 characters)*

> Hi [Name], I saw [Company] is hiring for [Role]. I have [X] years in [Field] and would love to connect ahead of applying.
> 

*(121 characters)*

> Hi [Name], your posts on [Topic] have been genuinely helpful. Would love to connect and keep learning from your work.
> 

*(117 characters)*

**Tips for staying under 200 characters:**

- Skip the pleasantries ("I hope you're doing well") — go straight to the reason
- One reason to connect, not three
- Swap [Name], [Role], [Company], [Field] for the real details — short names keep you well within the limit
- Never send the blank default note — personalized requests get accepted far more often

### Action 3 — Comment on 5 Posts (5 minutes)

Find posts in your field using relevant hashtags (#datajobs, #techjobs, #hiring, #dataanalytics, etc.) and leave genuine comments.

**A good comment:**

- Adds a perspective ("I've noticed this too, especially in banking environments")
- Asks a question ("How did you handle X in this situation?")
- Shares a resource ("I've seen this covered well in [course/article]")

**A bad comment:**

- "Great post!"
- "Thanks for sharing"
- Generic one-liners

## Week-by-Week Expectations

| Week | What to Expect |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Profile views increase slightly. A few connection acceptances. |
| Week 2 | Some post engagement starts. Recruiter views begin. |
| Week 3 | First recruiter messages or InMails if your profile is optimized. Comments from your network increase. |
| Week 4 | Noticeable increase in profile views, connection growth, and job recommendations from LinkedIn. |
